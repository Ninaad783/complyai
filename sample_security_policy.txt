COMPANY INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY 2025
SECTION 1: ACCESS CONTROL
All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) on all company systems. Passwords must be at least 16 characters and rotated every 90 days.
SECTION 2: DATA RETENTION & PRIVACY
Customer personal data must not be retained longer than 365 days after account closure. All data at rest must be encrypted using AES-256.
SECTION 3: MANDATORY COMPLIANCE TRAINING
Every employee in all departments must complete annual Cybersecurity Awareness Training by November 30th. Failure to complete results in suspension of network access.
